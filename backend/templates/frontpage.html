Our Mission

PolarGigs is a website dedicated to connecting Bowdoin students for service related transactions. As students at Bowdoin, we know how hard it can be to get access to services here, which is why we created PolarGigs. Whether you need a haircut, ride, or anything really, there's a great chance that we can connect you with another Bear that can help out!
